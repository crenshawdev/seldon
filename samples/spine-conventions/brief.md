# Toolshed repair log

## Problem
When a tool comes back broken, the volunteer on the counter leaves a note for the coordinator, the notes go missing, and broken tools go back out to members.
> when a tool comes back broken, the volunteer on the counter writes it on a sticky note for the coordinator, half the notes get lost, and broken drills go back on the shelf and out to the next member

## Solution
A repair log in Toolshed, kept by the coordinator alone, that holds each broken tool and its fault, and keeps the tool off the shelf until she marks it fixed.
> a repair log in Toolshed that the coordinator alone keeps: each tool that comes back broken, what's wrong with it, and when it's fixed
> A tool in the log stays off the shelf until the coordinator marks it fixed.

## Who it serves
The library's coordinator, the one person who repairs tools or writes them off.
> the library's coordinator, the one person who repairs tools or decides they're beyond saving

## What's different
A fault stays with its tool until it's fixed, instead of living on a note.
> today the only record of a fault is a note that falls off the counter, and the log keeps the fault with the tool until it's fixed

## Success criteria
- No tool the log holds as broken goes out to a member.
  > no tool the log holds as broken goes out to a member
- The coordinator sees every tool waiting for repair in one list.
  > the coordinator sees everything waiting for repair in one list

## Scope
Logging a broken tool and its fault, marking it fixed, and keeping it off the shelf until then.
> logging a broken tool and what's wrong with it, marking it fixed, and keeping it off the shelf until then

## What it refuses
- It won't track repair costs or parts, because the library doesn't pay for repairs and nobody wants to keep accounts.
  > It won't track what repairs cost or which parts they took, because the library doesn't pay for repairs and nobody wants to keep accounts.

## Vision
The coordinator knows which tools are broken without asking anyone.
> the coordinator knows which tools are broken without asking anyone
