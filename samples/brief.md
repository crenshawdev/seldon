# Toolshed

## Problem
A community tool library lends drills, ladders and saws to its members from a paper logbook. Tools go missing because nobody can tell who has what, or when it's due back.
> we lend drills, ladders, saws, all of it, out of a paper logbook
> and stuff walks off because nobody knows who has what or when it's due

## Solution
A small web app the volunteers use at the counter to check tools out and in, with a catalog members can browse from home.

## Who it serves
The volunteers who run the counter, and the few hundred members who borrow.

## What's different
It records every checkout and return as it happens, so where a tool is comes from the record and nobody types it in by hand.

## Success criteria
- A checkout at the counter takes under a minute.
- Overdue tools drop by half in the first three months.

## Scope
The catalog, checkouts, returns and due-date reminders.

## What it refuses
- It won't charge fees, because the library is free to its members and handling money is work the volunteers don't have time for.
- It won't take reservations, because a tool goes to whoever is at the counter, first come first served.
  > no reservations. whoever's standing at the counter gets the tool, first come first served
- It won't let members check tools out themselves, because a volunteer looks each tool over as it goes out and comes back.

## Vision
Any volunteer-run lending library can track what it lends without a logbook.
