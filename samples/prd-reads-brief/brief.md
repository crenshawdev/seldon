# Kilnshare

## Problem
A community pottery studio books its kiln loads on a whiteboard, and fired pieces pile up on the pickup shelf because nobody knows whose they are or how long they've sat there.
> we book the kilns on a whiteboard that gets wiped by accident
> and the pickup shelf is a mess, nobody knows whose pots are whose or how long they've been sitting

## Solution
A small web app where members book a spot in the next firing and hear when their pieces come out of the kiln.
> an app where members book a spot in the next firing and get a message when their stuff comes out

## Who it serves
The studio's members, and the two technicians who load and unload the kilns.
> our members, and the two techs who load and unload

## What's different
It ties every piece on the pickup shelf to the firing it came out of, so the shelf sorts itself by owner and by date. [ASSUMPTION]

## Success criteria
- A member books a spot in a firing in a couple of minutes at most.
  > booking a spot should take a couple of minutes, tops
- The pickup shelf is clear at the end of every month. [ASSUMPTION]

## Scope
Booking kiln loads, the message that pieces are ready, and the pickup shelf, where a fired piece waits 10 days for its owner before it goes to the free table.
> booking kiln loads, the ready message, and the pickup shelf
> fired pieces wait on the pickup shelf for 10 days, then they go to the free table

## What it refuses
- It won't charge for firings, because firing is part of the membership and nobody pays per load.
  > firing is part of membership, we don't charge per load
- It won't let members load a kiln themselves, because only the technicians are trained to load one safely.
  > only the techs load the kilns, it's a safety thing

## Vision
Any shared studio can run its kilns and its pickup shelf without a whiteboard. [ASSUMPTION]
