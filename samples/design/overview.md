# Quenlary: design overview

Quenlary keeps the watering rota for a shared allotment garden of about forty plots.

## Why

In a dry spell the shared beds and the seedling greenhouse need watering every evening. The rota lives on a whiteboard in the shed, so nobody can check it from home, and swaps made by text never reach the board.

## Decisions so far

- Plot holders sign up for evenings from their phones. No app to install, just a web page.
- A swap is one tap: offer your evening, and the first person to take it gets it.
- The committee sets which weeks the rota runs. Outside those weeks it's switched off.
- No accounts with passwords. Each plot holder gets a sign-in link by email.

## Open

- Whether to remind people the afternoon of their evening, or the night before.
