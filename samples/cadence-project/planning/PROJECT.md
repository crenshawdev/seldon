# Toolshed

## What This Is

Toolshed is a small web app for a volunteer-run tool library. The volunteers on the counter check drills, ladders and saws out to members and back in, and members browse the catalog from home. It replaced a paper logbook that couldn't say who had which tool or when it was due back.

## Core Value

Where every tool is comes from the checkout record, so nobody has to keep a logbook up to date by hand.

## Requirements

### Validated

- [x] A volunteer adds a tool to the catalog with a name, a category and a photo.
- [x] A member searches the catalog by name or category.
- [x] A volunteer checks a tool out to a member with a due date, in under a minute.
- [x] A volunteer checks a tool back in.
- [x] A member gets an email the day before a loan is due.

### Active

(None - every phase is built)

### Out of Scope

- Fees - the library is free to members, and nobody has time to handle money.
- Reservations - a tool goes to whoever is at the counter.
- Self checkout - a volunteer looks each tool over going out and coming back.

## Context

The library lends to a few hundred members from one counter, staffed by volunteers in shifts. Toolshed went live at the end of its third phase.

## Constraints

- **Users**: volunteers at the counter and members at home, on phones as often as laptops.
- **Cost**: free to members, so nothing in it takes payment.
