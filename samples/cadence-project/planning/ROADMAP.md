# Roadmap: Toolshed

## Overview

Toolshed was built in three phases: the catalog first, because every loan names a tool, then checkouts and returns at the counter, then the due-date reminders that run off those loans.

## Phases

- [x] **Phase 1: The catalog** - Volunteers add tools, and members search them
- [x] **Phase 2: Checkouts and returns** - Volunteers check tools out and back in at the counter
- [x] **Phase 3: Due-date reminders** - Members get an email the day before a loan is due

## Phase Details

### Phase 1: The catalog
**Goal:** Volunteers keep the list of tools, and members find one by name or category from home.
**Depends on:** Nothing
**Success Criteria:**
1. A volunteer adds a tool with a name, a category and a photo.
2. A member finds that tool by searching for its name or its category.

### Phase 2: Checkouts and returns
**Goal:** The counter runs on Toolshed instead of the logbook.
**Depends on:** Phase 1
**Success Criteria:**
1. A volunteer checks a tool out to a member with a due date in under a minute.
2. A volunteer checks the tool back in, and the catalog shows it on the shelf again.

### Phase 3: Due-date reminders
**Goal:** Members hear about a loan before it's late.
**Depends on:** Phase 2
**Success Criteria:**
1. A member gets one email the day before a loan is due.
2. A tool checked back in early sends no reminder.
