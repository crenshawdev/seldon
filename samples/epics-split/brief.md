# Plotwise

## Problem
An allotment society keeps its waiting list and its plot records in one shared spreadsheet that only the secretary can read. People waiting for a plot never know where they stand, and the committee loses track of who rents which plot and who has paid.
> people email me every week asking where they are on the waiting list
> the spreadsheet has who rents which plot and who paid, and only I can read it

## Solution
A web app with a public waiting list anyone can join, and a committee area where the society keeps its plots, who rents them, the rent and the inspections.
> a page where people can join the waiting list and see their place themselves
> one place for the committee to keep the plots, who rents them, the rent and the inspections, on a cheap hosted server

## Who it serves
The society's committee, who run the plots, and the people waiting for one, who only see the waiting list.
> the committee runs everything, the people waiting just need to see the list

## What's different
People waiting look up their own place on the list, so the secretary stops answering the same question every week. [ASSUMPTION]

## Success criteria
- The secretary stops getting emails asking about places on the waiting list.
  > I want to stop getting those emails
- Every plot's rent for the year is marked paid or unpaid by the end of March. [ASSUMPTION]

## Scope
The waiting list, the plots, who rents each one, the yearly rent and plot inspections.
> the waiting list, the plots, who rents them, the rent and the inspections

## What it refuses
- It won't take rent payments, because tenants pay the treasurer at the yearly meeting.
  > rent gets paid to the treasurer at the yearly meeting, no card payments
- It won't let anyone on the waiting list pick a plot, because the committee offers the next free plot in list order.
  > you can't choose your plot, you get offered the next one that's free

## Vision
Every allotment society runs its waiting list in the open. [ASSUMPTION]
