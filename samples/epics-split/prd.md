# Plotwise - PRD

## Requirements

### Lettings
- **PLOT-01**: Anyone joins the waiting list from a public page with their name and email.
  > a page where people can join the waiting list
- **PLOT-02**: Someone on the waiting list sees their own place on it.
  > see their place themselves
- **PLOT-03**: Someone on the waiting list takes themselves off it. [ASSUMPTION]
- **PLOT-04**: The committee keeps a record of every plot with its number and size.
  > one place for the committee to keep the plots
- **PLOT-05**: The committee lets a plot to a tenant from a start date.
  > who rents them
- **PLOT-06**: The committee marks each tenant's yearly rent as paid or unpaid.
  > who rents which plot and who paid
- **PLOT-07**: The committee records an inspection of a plot with its date and a note.
  > the inspections
- **PLOT-08**: The committee ends a tenancy and frees the plot. [ASSUMPTION]

## Non-functional requirements
- **NFR-01**: Joining the waiting list takes under two minutes on a phone. [ASSUMPTION]

## User journeys
A neighbour hears about the allotments, opens the public page on her phone and joins the waiting list. Each spring she checks her place on it. When she reaches the top, the committee lets her the next free plot, records her rent as paid after the yearly meeting and notes each inspection of her plot through the season.

## Non-goals
- It won't take rent payments, because tenants pay the treasurer at the yearly meeting.
  > rent gets paid to the treasurer at the yearly meeting, no card payments
- It won't let anyone on the waiting list pick a plot, because the committee offers the next free plot in list order.
  > you can't choose your plot, you get offered the next one that's free

## MVP scope
PLOT-01, PLOT-02 and PLOT-03.
