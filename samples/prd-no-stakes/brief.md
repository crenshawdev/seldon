# Readnext

## Problem
A book club picks its next book in a long email thread, and the suggestions get lost in the replies.
> we pick the next book over email and the suggestions get buried in the replies

## Solution
A page where members suggest books and vote on the next one.
> a page where people suggest books and vote on what we read next

## Who it serves
The members of a book club.
> it's for the book club

## What's different
Each vote closes on a set date, so the pick doesn't drag on. [ASSUMPTION]

## Success criteria
- The club picks its next book on the page instead of over email. [ASSUMPTION]

## Scope
Book suggestions and the vote on the next book.
> suggestions and the vote

## What it refuses
- It won't track who has read which book, because the club only cares what it reads next.
  > I don't care who's read what, just what we read next

## Vision
Book clubs pick their next read without an email thread. [ASSUMPTION]
