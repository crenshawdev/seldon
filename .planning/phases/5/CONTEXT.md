# Phase 5: Epics and the full walk - Context

Gathered: 2026-11-17

## Scope boundary

In: The epics step of `/seldon`. It reads `prd.md` and `spine.md` and writes `.planning/seldon/epics.md`: epics in build order, within the 5-requirement and 2-to-5 done-when limits, split along the spine's boundaries, each citing the spine decisions it relies on, checked by a new `--epics` mode that enforces the limits. Also in: the epics rerun (FLOW-03's epics half), the routes that reach the epics step on resume and where the walk ends once all four documents exist, `docs/FORMAT.md`, `samples/epics.md` and the README updated to match, the `.planning/CAPTURE.md` phase 5 todo (every epic shippable on its own), and the eval cases that prove each of these, with `epics-later` and `resume-later` rewritten.
Out: Existing projects and carrying a PRD change through spine and epics (phase 6: FLOW-05, ARCH-03, CHECK-03, CHECK-04). The readiness check and the handoff command (phase 7: CHECK-01, CHECK-02, FLOW-08). Run-wide graders and `/cad-verify` running the whole suite (phase 8: EVAL-02, EVAL-04). Checking in `lib/check.mjs` that an ID an epic names is declared in another file. Any change to SKILL.md's `allowed-tools` or the README's eval grant. The Cadence-side reader of `.planning/seldon/`.
Deferred: None
Plan shape: multiple plans - planning splits the work into several plans inside this phase; plans that share `skills/seldon/SKILL.md` run in order.

## Durable decisions

- D-01 (limits in the check): The epic limits are format rules the shared epic rules enforce, so the full check and `--epics` both fail closed: at most 5 IDs on `**Requirements:**`, 2 to 5 done-when items, and `**Depends on:**` naming only epics earlier in the file. `docs/FORMAT.md`'s "It doesn't size epics or trace requirements into epics" and "The format check reads the spelling of these IDs only" change to match. Rejected: grading the limits only by eval regex, which lets an oversized epic pass the step's own check and reach Cadence. Evidence: `lib/check.mjs` (checkEpic checks spelling, emptiness and order only); `docs/FORMAT.md` (lines 184, 317); phase 4 D-01; the analyzer's probe (an epic with 8 requirement IDs, 7 done-when items, `Depends on: E-03` and `S-99` passed the full check with exit 0).
- D-02 (shippable epics): Every epic is shippable on its own. Its done-when carries one item in fixed wording that verifies the epic end to end with the epics before it, plus at least one item of its own, so done-when holds 2 to 5 items, matching `/cad-new-project`'s 2 to 5 success criteria. A regex grades the fixed item. Rejected: the rule in SKILL.md only with no grader, and a floor of 2 with no integrated item. Evidence: `.planning/CAPTURE.md` (the phase 5 todo); `.planning/PROJECT.md` (Cadence's 2 to 5 success criteria; the done-when cap row); `samples/epics.md` line 27 (E-03 has one item); phase 3 D-21; owner's answer.
- D-03 (done-when shape): Done-when is always a `- ` list under `**Done when:**`, one item per bullet. The one-line inline form goes, because a floor of 2 can't be met on one line. `docs/FORMAT.md` states the counting rule and its examples change with it. Evidence: `samples/epics.md` (lines 6-8 bulleted, line 27 inline, the only inline one); `docs/FORMAT.md` (line 172 and the epic examples); D-02.
- D-04 (one epic per requirement): Every PRD requirement ID lands in exactly one epic, and NFR IDs count toward the 5-ID limit, riding with the epic whose capability they constrain. Rejected: repeating a cross-cutting NFR in every epic it constrains, outside the count. Evidence: `.planning/REQUIREMENTS.md` line 8 (each maps to exactly one roadmap phase); `samples/epics.md` line 21 (E-02 carries NFR-01 and NFR-02); `docs/FORMAT.md` line 101 (NFR IDs share the requirement ID space); `.planning/PROJECT.md` (Cadence builds REQUIREMENTS from the PRD and the roadmap from the epics).
- D-05 (no quotes in epics): Epics don't follow the quote-or-tag rule. An epic's source is its `**Requirements:**` and `**Spine decisions:**` IDs. `[ASSUMPTION]` stays allowed, not required, in Outcome, Boundaries and Done when. Rejected: quote-or-tag on Outcome, which needs brief lines the step never reads. Evidence: `docs/FORMAT.md` ("This rule covers `brief.md`, `prd.md` and `spine.md`"; line 219, where the tag is allowed); `samples/epics.md` (no quotes or tags); phase 2 D-18, phase 3 D-13, phase 4 D-03.
- D-06 (build order): File order is build order, and `docs/FORMAT.md` says so, so Cadence's reader is built against it. An epic depends only on epics before it, and MVP-scope requirements come before the rest. A split-off epic takes the next unused number wherever it sits, so E- numbers need not ascend through the file. Rejected: new epics only appended at the end, which puts a split-off epic after the epics that depend on it. Evidence: `docs/FORMAT.md` (lines 152, 184); `samples/prd.md` line 34 and `samples/epics.md` (MVP IDs in E-01 and E-02, LOAN-03 in E-03); `lib/check.mjs` (ID order isn't checked); owner's answer.
- D-07 (capability and split): A source capability is one PRD `### ` category, and a tightly coupled pair is two. NFRs aren't a capability. An epic over a limit or covering more than one capability is split along a `Kind: boundaries` spine decision, and every epic on either side cites that decision's S- ID in `**Spine decisions:**`. Rejected: only the half across the boundary citing it, as `samples/epics.md` does today, which gives a grader no signal on the other half. Evidence: `skills/seldon/SKILL.md` line 621 (every requirement sits under a `### <Category>`); `samples/prd.md`, `samples/epics.md` (Loans split into E-02 and E-03 along S-03, cited only by E-03); `samples/spine.md` line 14 (S-03 is `Kind: boundaries`); `.planning/PROJECT.md` (the boundary rule).
- D-08 (no boundary to split on): When a capability is over a limit and no `Kind: boundaries` decision cuts it, the epics step writes nothing, names the capability, and points to `/seldon spine` to add a boundary first. It asks no question, so it needs no route back. Rejected: splitting anyway with Boundaries tagged `[ASSUMPTION]`, which creates a boundary no S- ID locks, and a question turn with a new section-1 route. Evidence: `.planning/REQUIREMENTS.md` EPIC-02, CHECK-01; phase 4 D-07 (`/seldon spine` with no PRD writes nothing and says so); `.planning/phases/3/SUMMARY.md` (stop points with no route back); owner's answer.
- D-09 (citations): Every S- ID in the spine is cited by at least one epic, and every epic cites every stack, hosting and design-approach decision, so each Cadence phase locks them through `/cad-context`. Rejected: project-wide decisions cited only by the first epic, and no citing rule. Evidence: `.planning/PROJECT.md` (`/cad-context` brings in each epic's spine decisions as locked decisions); `samples/epics.md` (S-04 stack, S-05 hosting and S-06 boundaries are cited by no epic); `samples/spine.md` (S-02 design approach, S-04 stack, S-05 hosting); owner's answer.
- D-10 (epics rerun): `/seldon epics` over an existing `epics.md` is a rerun, the way the spine rerun works. It reads the previous `epics.md` first, changes it with Edit only, keeps every epic the change doesn't touch byte-identical, keeps a changed epic's ID, lists a dropped epic's ID on a prose `Retired: E-03, ...` line directly under the `# ` title, and never reuses a retired ID. `docs/FORMAT.md` gains that line for `epics.md`. FLOW-03's traceability row flips to Complete at phase 5's close through `planning.mjs`, since both the spine and epics reruns then exist. Rejected: the `Retired:` line after the last epic, which the check reads as part of that epic's last field. Evidence: phase 4 D-04, D-16; `docs/FORMAT.md` (lines 186-209: `Retired:` is defined for the PRD and spine only); `.planning/REQUIREMENTS.md` (FLOW-03 row); the analyzer's probe (`Retired: E-07` under the title passes today's full check).
