Resume after a `/clear` from the files in `.planning/seldon/`. Rerunning one step with `/seldon brief` or `/seldon prd`, keeping unchanged requirements and retiring dropped IDs. A PRD-only mode for `lib/check.mjs` that also requires ID'd requirement bullets, with `docs/FORMAT.md`, `samples/prd.md` and the tests updated to match. The two queued phase 3 todos: the PRD carries the brief's refusals beside its non-goals, and the PRD keeps verbatim quotes. New eval cases proving AC1 to AC6, the existing-brief case and README's release text rewritten, and the fixtures they need under `samples/`.

Marking later documents stale after an earlier step reruns, and showing the decisions a PRD change conflicts with (phase 6, CHECK-03 and CHECK-04). Any change to SKILL.md's `allowed-tools` (phase 2 D-02 stands).
Deferred: None
Plan shape: multiple plans - several plans inside this phase; plans that share `skills/seldon/SKILL.md` run in order.

## Durable decisions

- D-01 (confirmation): A step's document on disk counts as confirmed. FORMAT.md's four files carry no confirmation record. On resume, Seldon first names the file it is building from, before anything is drafted, and says `/seldon brief` (or the step's name) revises it. A user who clears before confirming has chosen to, and still sees what was carried forward. Evidence: `docs/FORMAT.md` (exactly four files, only named sections); phase 1 D-01; mother's ruling for John.
- D-02 (a non-step argument when documents exist): When `$ARGUMENTS` isn't a step name and documents already exist, such as a new dump while `brief.md` is there, Seldon writes nothing and asks in one prose question whether to rerun the brief with this dump (`/seldon brief`) or go on from the brief on disk. The user's words are never set aside silently. Evidence: `evals/existing-brief/`; mother's ruling for John.
- D-03 (retired IDs): A requirement a rerun drops has its ID listed on a prose `Retired: CAT-03, ...` line under `## Requirements`, and no later rerun reuses a retired ID, because IDs carry into Cadence's REQUIREMENTS.md unchanged (phase 1 D-02). Evidence: `docs/FORMAT.md` (a removed ID stays retired); `lib/check.mjs` (only `- ` bullets in Requirements and Non-functional requirements are read; the analyzer's probe passed a `Retired:` line with exit 0).
- D-04 (`/seldon brief`): Rerunning the brief reopens the brief step on the existing `brief.md` and updates it in place, keeping every untouched item and its quote byte-identical, and leaves `prd.md` alone. Marking the PRD stale after a brief change is phase 6's CHECK-04. Evidence: `.planning/REQUIREMENTS.md` FLOW-03, CHECK-04.
- D-05 (stakes): The PRD step infers the stakes from the brief's Who it serves, Success criteria and Scope, and sizes the PRD to them. When the brief doesn't show the stakes, the sizing is tagged `[ASSUMPTION]` in the PRD, with no extra question turn and no change to the brief's format. Evidence: `docs/FORMAT.md` (the brief has no stakes section); `.planning/REQUIREMENTS.md` DOCS-02.
- D-06 (where a run starts): On a new run, the files in `.planning/seldon/` decide where `/seldon` starts, and the conversation decides only inside a step already under way. This replaces SKILL.md §1's "read only the conversation" rule and phase 2's D-08 brief-exists stop and its fixed "later release" line. Evidence: `skills/seldon/SKILL.md` §1 and the brief-exists stop; phase 2 D-08.
- D-07 (ending a step): A step writes its document, runs the check, repeats the check's result line, and asks the user to confirm or name changes. When the user confirms, Seldon writes nothing more and ends on fixed words that suggest `/clear` and then `/seldon`, fixed because phase 2's model paraphrased free wording past its graders three times. This replaces the "run finished" position. Evidence: `.planning/phases/2/SUMMARY.md` (the fixed-words deviations); `skills/seldon/SKILL.md`.
- D-08 (the rerun argument): `$ARGUMENTS` is a rerun only when the whole trimmed argument is exactly `brief`, `prd`, `spine` or `epics`. Anything else keeps its current meaning: the dump on a new run, or a summary or answer on a later turn (D-02 covers a dump when documents exist). Evidence: `skills/seldon/SKILL.md`; phase 2 D-15 (continuation prompts are `/seldon <answer>`).
- D-09 (keeping IDs): A rerun reads the previous version of its own document, and every requirement the change doesn't touch keeps its line byte-identical: the ID, the text and its quote lines. Evidence: `docs/FORMAT.md` (IDs are stable); `.planning/ROADMAP.md` phase 3 criterion 4.
- D-10 (check mode): `lib/check.mjs` gains a PRD-only mode mirroring `--brief`, with its own result line, because right after the PRD step the full check can only fail on the missing spine and epics. Evidence: `lib/check.mjs`; phase 2 D-05.
- D-11 (PRD check): The PRD check requires at least one ID'd `- **CAT-01**:` bullet in `## Requirements` and one in `## Non-functional requirements`. Today a PRD with only prose there passes. Evidence: `lib/check.mjs`; the analyzer's probe (prose-only sections passed with exit 0).
- D-12 (refusals in the PRD): The brief's refusals carry into `## Non-goals` as `- <won't> because <reason>` bullets, each with the brief's quote under it. The PRD keeps its five sections. Evidence: the phase 3 refusals todo in `.planning/CAPTURE.md`; phase 2 D-17; `docs/FORMAT.md` PRD section.
- D-13 (PRD quotes): The PRD's quote sources are the brief's `> ` lines, keeping a design-file line where the brief's quote had one, plus the user's replies during the PRD step. Every requirement and non-goal line carries a `> ` quote under it or an `[ASSUMPTION]` tag, and the tag never sits inside the bold ID. Evidence: the phase 3 verbatim-answers todo in `.planning/CAPTURE.md`; phase 2 D-18; `docs/FORMAT.md` ("The PRD and spine take the same rule when their steps are built").
- D-14 (no technology in the PRD): The PRD names no library, framework, format or hosting choice, even when the brief does, because those belong to the spine. A tech word the user said stays in the brief and isn't carried into the PRD's quotes. Evidence: `.planning/ROADMAP.md` phase 3 criterion 1; `.planning/REQUIREMENTS.md` DOCS-02 ("capabilities, not implementation").
- D-15 (what the PRD step reads): The PRD step reads only `brief.md`, never `docs/design/` and never the dump. Design-folder facts reach the PRD only through the brief's sourced quotes. Evidence: `.planning/REQUIREMENTS.md` FLOW-04; `docs/FORMAT.md` quote section.

## Decisions

- D-16 (`/clear`): Seldon suggests a `/clear` and never requires one. `/seldon` in a conversation that still holds earlier talk builds the step from the files alone. Evidence: `.planning/REQUIREMENTS.md` FLOW-04 ("suggests"); `.planning/ROADMAP.md` phase 3 criterion 3.
- D-17 (later steps): Until phases 4 and 5 build them, a resume that finds both brief and PRD, and any `/seldon spine` or `/seldon epics`, writes nothing and ends on a fixed "later release" line. Evidence: `.planning/ROADMAP.md` phases 4 and 5.
- D-18 (permissions): The PRD step needs no new permission. `allowed-tools` stays phase 2 D-02's five entries, and only the body's "the only file you write is `brief.md`" widens to `prd.md`. Evidence: `skills/seldon/SKILL.md`; `tests/skill.test.mjs`.
- D-19 (no second recommendation): The PRD step doesn't recommend a path again. Running it is the go-ahead, and it drafts in the same turn unless something only the user can decide is open, in which case every such question goes in one prose turn (phase 2 D-01, D-09). Every brief section counts as answered, tagged guesses included. Evidence: `skills/seldon/SKILL.md`; `.planning/REQUIREMENTS.md` FLOW-07.
- D-20 (eval mechanics): The resume case starts a fresh session: a scaffold copies a confirmed brief from `samples/` into `.planning/seldon/`, and the prompt is a bare `/seldon`. New fixtures live in `samples/` subfolders under the scaffold allowlist, and `samples/brief.md` and `samples/prd.md` stay the one conforming set, updated in place for quotes and refusals. The existing-brief case and README's "In this release" text get rewritten for D-02 and D-06. Any new case that reuses design-folder's Bash grader must allow the PRD check mode too. Evidence: `tests/scaffold.test.mjs`; `evals/existing-brief/`; `evals/design-folder/graders/no-bash-but-check.md`; phase 2 D-12, D-15, D-16.
- D-21 (graders): Every new grader is checked against one question before it's committed: can it pass when the behavior it claims is missing? AC1 adapts phase 2's every-item grader to `prd.md`, closing the gap phase 2's verify found. Evidence: `.planning/phases/2/UAT.md` items 6 and 9; `evals/fast-path-brief/graders/every-item-quoted-or-tagged.md`; mother's ruling for John.
