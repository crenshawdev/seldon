/seldon Toolshed already runs our tool library's counter, and the next thing it needs is a repair log. Problem: when a tool comes back broken, the volunteer on the counter writes it on a sticky note for the coordinator, half the notes get lost, and broken drills go back on the shelf and out to the next member. Solution: a repair log in Toolshed that the coordinator alone keeps: each tool that comes back broken, what's wrong with it, and when it's fixed. A tool in the log stays off the shelf until the coordinator marks it fixed. It serves the library's coordinator, the one person who repairs tools or decides they're beyond saving. What's different: today the only record of a fault is a note that falls off the counter, and the log keeps the fault with the tool until it's fixed. Success: no tool the log holds as broken goes out to a member, and the coordinator sees everything waiting for repair in one list. Scope: logging a broken tool and what's wrong with it, marking it fixed, and keeping it off the shelf until then. It won't track what repairs cost or which parts they took, because the library doesn't pay for repairs and nobody wants to keep accounts. It won't let members report damage themselves, because a volunteer looks every tool over when it comes back, and that's where faults get found. Vision: the coordinator knows which tools are broken without asking anyone. Stakes are low: if the log is wrong, a broken tool goes out the way it does today, and the member brings it back.
