The short version: about forty of us share the garden, and in a dry spell the shared beds and the greenhouse need watering every evening. It's for the plot holders and the committee who run the rota. It's a volunteer thing with no money involved, and if it breaks we go back to the whiteboard. The notes say more about what I've decided so far.
